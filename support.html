Frequently Asked Questions

What is effct.?
effct. is a camera app with cinematic filters organized in two collections: Eras and Moods. Eras are inspired by the distinct color profiles of different decades, while Moods are designed to evoke specific emotions and atmospheres. Both collections include a variety of filters.
What does effct. Pro include?
Pro unlocks all premium filters, the ability to edit the filter on any photo in your gallery, and automatic saving to your Photo Library. Available as monthly, annual, or lifetime subscription.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open effct., tap the menu, and access the subscription screen. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and will restore automatically. If it doesn't, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find effct. and tap Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.
Are my photos stored on a server?
No. effct. has no server, no accounts, and no tracking. All your photos and videos stay on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.
Can I change the filter on a photo after taking it?
Yes, effct. saves your original photos without any filter baked in. You can change or remove the filter anytime from the gallery. Editing the filter on existing photos is a Pro feature.
What iOS version is required?
effct. requires iOS 26 or later.
